---
title: Glossary
description: The vocabulary used across Spreadless, defined in one place.
---

**AMM (Automated Market Maker)**: a smart contract that quotes and settles trades
from a pool of reserves using a fixed pricing rule, instead of matching buyers and
sellers in an order book.

**Amplification factor (A)**: the tunable coefficient in the StableSwap invariant
that sets how flat the curve is near parity. Higher `A` means less slippage while
balanced and a sharper penalty when imbalanced. See
[Amplification Factor](/docs/concepts/amplification).

**Constant-product curve**: the `x · y = k` pricing rule used by conventional
AMMs. Curved everywhere, so every trade has price impact. StableSwap approaches it
as `A → 0`.

**Constant-sum line**: the flat `x + y = D` rule: a perfect 1:1 exchange with zero
slippage but no defence against imbalance. StableSwap approaches it as `A → ∞`.

**D (invariant total)**: the invariant's measure of total pool value; roughly the
sum of reserves when balanced. Held constant (per the formula) through each trade.

**De-peg**: when a stablecoin trades away from its intended value. The main risk to
a liquidity position, since the pool accumulates the weaker asset.

**Horizon**: Stellar's classic-layer API. Used for classic operations like
[trustlines](/docs/guides/trustlines), which live outside Soroban.

**Invariant**: the mathematical rule relating a pool's reserves that must stay
constant across trades. Its shape determines all pricing. See
[The Invariant Curve](/docs/concepts/invariant-curve).

**LP (Liquidity Provider)**: someone who deposits assets into a pool to earn a
share of its trading fees.

**LP shares / `SLP`**: the token representing a claim on a pool. On Spreadless the
pool contract *is* the LP token; `SLP` uses 9 decimals.

**Minimum received / `min_out`**: the on-chain floor a write enforces, quote minus
tolerance. The transaction reverts rather than filling below it. See
[Slippage & Safety](/docs/protocol/slippage-and-safety).

**Price impact**: how much your own trade moves the price against you, from walking
along the curve as you trade.

**Raw units**: on-chain integer amounts in a token's smallest unit. Scale by the
token's decimals to get human values.

**SAC (Stellar Asset Contract)**: a Soroban contract wrapping a *classic* Stellar
asset. Holding one requires a [trustline](/docs/guides/trustlines); only its issuer
can mint it.

**Single-sided liquidity**: depositing one asset into a multi-asset pool without
pairing. Possible because the invariant assumes correlated assets. See
[Single-Sided Liquidity](/docs/concepts/single-sided).

**Slippage**: the gap between the price you expected and the price you executed at;
price impact plus any quote-to-settlement drift.

**Soroban**: Stellar's smart-contract platform, where the pool contract runs.

**StableSwap**: the invariant (originating with Curve) that blends constant-sum and
constant-product behaviour, staying flat near parity and steepening off it. The core
of Spreadless pricing.

**Tolerance**: the slippage allowance you accept, subtracted from the quote to set
the on-chain floor. Swaps use parts-per-million; deposits/withdrawals use basis
points.

**Trustline**: a Stellar account's explicit opt-in to hold a specific classic
asset. See [Trustlines](/docs/guides/trustlines).

**TVL (Total Value Locked)**: the total value of reserves held across pools.
