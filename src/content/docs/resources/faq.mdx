## Is this real money?

No. Everything documented here runs on **Stellar Testnet** with test tokens that
have no value. Never send mainnet funds to any address in these docs.

## Why is my swap's price impact so low?

Because Spreadless prices with a [StableSwap invariant](/docs/concepts/invariant-curve)
that stays flat near a 1:1 exchange for correlated assets. On a balanced pool your
trade barely moves the price — that's the entire design goal. Push the pool far off
balance and it steepens quickly.

## What is the amplification factor?

`A` is the dial that sets how flat the curve is near the peg. Higher `A` = tighter
pricing and less slippage while balanced, but a sharper penalty once imbalanced.
See [Amplification Factor](/docs/concepts/amplification) — it has an interactive
model.

## Can I really deposit just one token?

Yes. [Single-sided liquidity](/docs/concepts/single-sided) works because the
invariant already assumes the assets trade near parity, so the pool can value an
imbalanced contribution fairly. You receive `SLP` LP shares and can withdraw into
any single asset later.

## What's an LP token here?

`SLP`, 9 decimals. Unusually, **the pool contract is itself the LP token** — there's
no separate deployment. Your share balance is read from the pool's own `balance()`.
See [Architecture](/docs/protocol/architecture).

## Why did an asset ask me for a "trustline"?

That asset is a classic Stellar asset (a Stellar Asset Contract). Stellar requires
your account to opt into holding a classic asset via a
[trustline](/docs/guides/trustlines) first. The app adds it in one click. Native
tokens don't need one.

## Is the "minimum received" a real guarantee?

Yes. It's the exact `min_out` the contract enforces on-chain — your quote minus
tolerance. If the pool can't deliver at least that, the transaction reverts. See
[Slippage & Safety](/docs/protocol/slippage-and-safety).

## Where does the APY come from, and is it fixed?

From trading fees accruing to the pool, split across LP shares. It is **not fixed**
— the displayed figure is an annualised projection from recent activity and moves
with volume and liquidity. See [Fees & Yield](/docs/concepts/fees-yield).

## What's the biggest risk as a liquidity provider?

A **de-peg**. The invariant keeps quoting near 1:1 into a failing peg, so the pool
accumulates the weak asset — the stablecoin form of impermanent loss. The
execution floor protects your trade price, not the assets' pegs.

## Is it custodial?

No. Spreadless is non-custodial; the contract only moves funds you authorise in a
signed transaction, and every action is a verifiable on-chain Stellar transaction.
