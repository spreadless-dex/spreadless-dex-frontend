<Callout kind="note" title="SAC vs native">
  A **Stellar Asset Contract** wraps a *classic* asset, so it can only be held by
  an account with a trustline for it, and only its issuer can mint it. Native
  Soroban test tokens have neither constraint; anyone can mint them from the
  faucet and hold them without a trustline. This is why `SUSD` behaves differently
  from the other three throughout the app.
</Callout>

## Verifying an address

For any Stellar Asset Contract, `Asset(code, issuer).contractId(passphrase)` must
equal the token's contract address, a check you can run yourself to confirm a SAC
wraps the classic asset it claims to. Contract and transaction links throughout the
app point at Stellar Expert on this network so anything can be independently
inspected.
