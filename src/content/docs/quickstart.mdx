This is the fastest path through the whole product. Every step links to a
detailed guide if you want more.

<Callout kind="note" title="Testnet only">
  You will use test tokens with no real value. Never send mainnet funds to any
  address here.
</Callout>

## 1. Connect a wallet

Click **Connect Wallet** in the top-right and pick a Stellar wallet (Freighter is
the simplest browser option). Approve the connection. A brand-new testnet account
needs a little test XLM for fees first — fund it from Friendbot (most wallets,
Freighter included, have a one-click button); actions revert until it's funded.
→ [Connect a Wallet](/docs/guides/connect-wallet)

## 2. Get test tokens

Open the **[Faucet](/faucet)** and mint a couple of the open-mint stablecoins
(for example `USDx` and `PYUSD`). These arrive in your wallet in a few seconds.
→ [Get Testnet Tokens](/docs/guides/faucet)

## 3. Add a trustline (only if prompted)

Some assets are *classic* Stellar assets and require a **trustline** before your
account can hold them. If you try to receive one without a trustline, Spreadless
offers to add it in one click.
→ [Trustlines](/docs/guides/trustlines)

## 4. Swap

Go to **[Swap](/swap)**, choose what you're paying and receiving, type an amount,
and review the quote — rate, price impact, network fee, and minimum received.
Hit swap and sign. Settlement is a few seconds.
→ [How to Swap](/docs/guides/swap)

## 5. Earn (optional)

Head to **[Earn](/earn)** and deposit a *single* stablecoin into a pool to receive
LP shares and start earning a slice of trading fees. Withdraw back into any one
asset whenever you like.
→ [Provide Liquidity](/docs/guides/provide-liquidity)

---

That's the entire loop: **connect → fund → swap → earn**. If you want to
understand *why* the swap barely moved the price, read
[The Invariant Curve](/docs/concepts/invariant-curve) next.
