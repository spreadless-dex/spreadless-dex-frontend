Once you're trading and providing liquidity, two views keep track of it: your
**positions** and your **activity history**.

## Your positions

The portfolio view shows the pools you're in, your `SLP` share balance, what it's
currently worth, and quick access to deposit more or withdraw.

<Figure
  src="/docs/screenshots/pools.png"
  alt="The pools list showing each pool with its assets, TVL and APY"
  caption="The pools list: assets, TVL, and APY at a glance. Open one for the full breakdown."
/>

Each pool also has its own detail page with a **Parameters & Health** panel
(the pool's amplification `A`, type, status, LP supply) and a **composition** bar
showing how balanced the reserves currently are. That balance is what determines
your [slippage](/docs/concepts/slippage) and the cost of single-sided
deposits/withdrawals.

<Figure
  src="/docs/screenshots/pool-detail.png"
  alt="A pool detail page with composition bar and a Parameters and Health panel"
  caption="A pool's detail page: composition across all four assets, plus amplification, status, and LP supply."
/>

## Activity history

The **[Activity](/activity)** page lists your swaps, deposits, and withdrawals,
each linking out to the transaction on a block explorer so anything can be
independently verified.

<Figure
  alt="An activity feed of swaps, deposits and withdrawals with explorer links"
  caption="Every action, with a link to verify it on-chain."
/>

<Callout kind="note" title="Everything is verifiable">
  Spreadless is non-custodial and on-chain. Every position and every history entry
  corresponds to a real Stellar transaction you can inspect on
  [Stellar Expert](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet). The app is a view
  onto the chain, not the source of truth.
</Callout>

That's the full user journey. To go deeper into how it all works on-chain, read
the [Protocol](/docs/protocol/architecture) section.
