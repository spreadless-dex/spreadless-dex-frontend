A **trustline** is a Stellar-specific concept with no equivalent on most other
chains. Before your account can hold a *classic* Stellar asset, it must explicitly
opt in to that asset by creating a trustline for it. Without one, any transfer of
that asset into your account simply fails.

## When you'll hit one

Most test tokens here are native Soroban tokens and need no trustline. The
exception in the current deployment is **`SUSD`**, which is a Stellar Asset
Contract wrapping a classic asset. If you try to receive `SUSD` — for example by
swapping into it or withdrawing a pool into it — without a trustline, the transfer
can't land.

Spreadless detects this and offers to add the trustline first, inline, before the
swap or withdrawal:

## Adding one

1. Attempt the action that needs the asset (swap into it, or withdraw into it).
2. When the trustline notice appears, click to **add the trustline**.
3. Approve the transaction in your wallet.
4. Retry the original action — it now goes through.

<Callout kind="note" title="It's a classic-layer transaction">
  Trustlines live on the classic Stellar account, not inside Soroban, so adding
  one is a classic transaction that goes through Horizon rather than the smart
  contract. It's a one-time step per asset, and costs only the tiny base fee plus
  a small reserve that's released if you ever remove the trustline.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip" title="Native tokens skip all this">
  `USDx`, `PYUSD`, and `sUSDC` are native and need no trustline — you can swap and
  deposit them immediately after [minting](/docs/guides/faucet).
</Callout>
