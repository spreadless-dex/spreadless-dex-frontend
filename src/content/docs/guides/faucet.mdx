To try swaps and deposits you need some test tokens. The built-in **[Faucet](/faucet)**
mints them straight to your connected wallet, free, on Stellar Testnet.

<Figure
  src="/docs/screenshots/faucet.png"

## Steps

1. [Connect your wallet](/docs/guides/connect-wallet) if you haven't.
2. Open the **[Faucet](/faucet)**.
3. Choose a token, enter an amount (or accept the default), and click
   **Mint {"{token}"}**.
4. Approve the transaction in your wallet. The balance lands in a few seconds.

## Which tokens are mintable

Only the **open-mint** test tokens can be minted from the faucet. These are test
assets whose `mint(to, amount)` is open to anyone. In the current testnet
deployment those are `USDx`, `PYUSD`, and `sUSDC`.

<Callout kind="note" title="Why SUSD isn't on the faucet">
  `SUSD` is a **Stellar Asset Contract** wrapping a *classic* asset, so only its
  issuer can mint it, so it isn't faucet-mintable. Holding it also requires a
  [trustline](/docs/guides/trustlines) first. The other three are native test
  tokens with none of that ceremony.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="warning" title="No value">
  These are testnet tokens. They are not redeemable and have no monetary value.
  They exist only to exercise the app.
</Callout>

Now that you're funded, go [make a swap](/docs/guides/swap).
