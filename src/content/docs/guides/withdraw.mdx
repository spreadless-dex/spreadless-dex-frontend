Your liquidity isn't locked. Whenever you like, burn some or all of your `SLP`
shares and take the value out as **one** asset of your choice from the pool.

<Figure
  alt="The withdraw panel with an amount-to-burn field and the asset to exit into"
  caption="Withdrawing: choose how much LP to burn and which asset to receive."
/>

## Steps

1. Open **[Earn](/earn)** and select the pool you're in (or find the position in
   your [Portfolio](/docs/guides/portfolio)).
2. On the **Withdraw** tab, set how much LP to burn. Use **Max** to exit fully.
3. Choose the asset to receive. The panel quotes the amount out from a live
   simulation.
4. Confirm and approve in your wallet.

## Exit into any single asset

Withdrawal uses `withdraw_one_token`: you burn LP and receive the entire value in
**one** chosen asset, not a basket. So you can enter a pool with `USDx` and leave
entirely in `PYUSD` if that's what you need.

<Callout kind="note" title="Trustline for classic assets">
  If you exit into a classic asset like `SUSD`, your account needs a
  [trustline](/docs/guides/trustlines) for it first. The app offers to add it
  inline before the withdrawal.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip" title="Withdrawing one token walks the curve">
  Pulling everything into a single asset nudges the pool off balance, so it carries
  a small price impact, the same curve mechanics as a swap. Exiting into the pool's
  *abundant* asset is cheaper than into its scarce one.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="warning" title="On-chain minimum">
  The withdrawal carries a `min_amount_out` floor from your quote minus tolerance.
  A moving pool can't pay you less than that. The contract reverts instead.
</Callout>
