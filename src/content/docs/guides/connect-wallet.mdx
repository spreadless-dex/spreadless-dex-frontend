Everything that touches the chain, from swapping to depositing to minting from
the faucet, needs a connected Stellar wallet to sign for you. Connecting is read-and-sign
only: Spreadless never sees your secret key, and no transaction leaves your wallet
without your explicit approval.

## Connect

Click **Connect Wallet** in the top-right of the header. You are asked how you
want to connect:

- **Browser wallet**: a wallet picker appears, listing the Stellar wallets
  available in your browser. You keep your own keys.
- **Email or Google**: sign in with an email address or a Google account. A
  Stellar account is created for you and funded with test XLM automatically.
  Nothing to install, so this is the way to use Spreadless on a phone.

Pick your wallet and approve the connection in its popup. Once connected, the
header button shows your account address, shortened (e.g. `GABC…7XYZ`). Clicking
it again disconnects. With the email login, transactions are approved through
your login session instead of a wallet popup.

## Supported wallets

Spreadless uses the Stellar Wallets Kit, so it works with the common Stellar
wallets. [**Freighter**](https://www.freighter.app/) is the simplest browser
extension to start with.

<Callout kind="note" title="Freighter on a phone">
  The Freighter mobile app cannot connect to Spreadless yet: it talks to sites
  through WalletConnect, which Spreadless does not offer at the moment. On a
  phone, use the **Email or Google** option instead.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip" title="First time on testnet">
  A brand-new testnet account needs a little test XLM to pay transaction fees.
  Most wallets can fund a testnet account from Friendbot in one click (Freighter
  shows a **Fund with Friendbot** button). Until the account is funded, the app
  can't submit anything: trustlines and swaps revert with an "account not funded"
  error. Do this before you go further.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="warning" title="Never share your secret key">
  Spreadless will never ask for your secret key or seed phrase. You approve each
  transaction inside your own wallet. If any page asks you to type a secret key,
  stop.
</Callout>
