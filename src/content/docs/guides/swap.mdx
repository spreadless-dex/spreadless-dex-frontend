Swapping is the core action: pay one stablecoin, receive another, priced by the
[invariant curve](/docs/concepts/invariant-curve). The panel shows the full
economics of the trade before you commit.

## Steps

1. Open **[Swap](/swap)**.
2. In **You pay**, pick the token you're selling and enter an amount, or use the
   **25 / 50 / 75 / Max** shortcuts to fill from your balance.
3. In **You receive**, pick the token you want. The quote fills in automatically.
4. Review the detail rows (below).
5. Click **Exchange** and approve in your wallet. Settlement takes a few seconds.

## Reading the quote

- **Exchange rate**: the effective rate for this trade. Tap it to invert the
  direction it's displayed in.
- **Price impact**: how much your trade moves the price, shown as `<0.01%` when
  tiny and flagged in red past ~1%. On balanced stablecoin pools it stays very
  small (this is the whole point; see [Slippage](/docs/concepts/slippage)).
- **Network fee**: the estimated Stellar fee, read from the simulation.
- **Execution protection → Minimum received**: the hard floor enforced on-chain.
  If the pool can't deliver at least this, the transaction reverts.

<Callout kind="tip" title="The minimum is real">
  *Minimum received* isn't a display estimate. It's the exact `min_out` the
  contract enforces. Your quote minus your slippage tolerance. You will never be
  filled below it.
</Callout>

## Button states

The action button tells you what's blocking a trade before you try:

- **Log in to exchange**: not logged in yet.
- **Enter an amount**: no input amount.
- **Insufficient {"{token}"} balance**: you don't hold enough of the pay token.
- **Insufficient liquidity**: the pool can't support a trade this large.
- **Exchange {"{from}"} → {"{to}"}**: good to go.

<Callout kind="note" title="Quotes need a connected wallet">
  The quote is a live on-chain simulation run against your account, so the rate,
  price impact, network fee, and minimum-received rows fill in only once a wallet is
  connected. For classic assets you'll also need a
  [trustline](/docs/guides/trustlines) before you can receive them.
</Callout>
