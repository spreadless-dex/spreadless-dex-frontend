Every pool has at most one owner. The owner is the only address that can ramp the
amplification factor `A`, change the swap fee, or pause the pool. The creator of
a pool never keeps this role for themselves. They decide at creation who holds it
instead.

## The choice at creation

Step 3 of the pool builder, **Right to change A**, has two options:

- **Flexible** (recommended). The pool is deployed with Spreadless as owner. If a
  market shifts, Spreadless can retune `A`, always as a slow ramp, never as a jump.
  One signature.
- **Fixed.** You deploy, then give ownership up in a second signature. The pool
  has no owner from then on. `A`, the fee and the pause switch are frozen for
  everyone, Spreadless included. This cannot be undone.

If you decline the second signature of a fixed deploy, the pool still exists and
you remain its owner. Its page shows **Undecided** until you make the choice
there.

## Handing a pool over

A pool you still own shows a **Transfer** button in the Owner row of its page.
It opens the same three choices:

1. **Spreadless.** Hand the pool over. The pool becomes flexible.
2. **Nobody, fix it for good.** Give ownership up. You press twice to confirm.
3. **Another wallet.** Offer the pool to any Stellar address, account (`G...`)
   or contract (`C...`).

A transfer to another wallet is a two-step handshake. You sign an **offer**. The
recipient signs an **accept**. Nothing moves until they do. The offer stays open
for seven days and then lapses. While it is open you can take it back, and the
pool page shows the offer with a copyable invite link. The recipient opens that
link with their wallet connected and finds the Accept step waiting.

<Callout kind="note" title="A wrong address costs nothing">
  Ownership only moves when the recipient accepts. An address nobody controls can
  never accept, so a mistyped offer simply lapses and the pool stays yours.
</Callout>

## What the states mean

| State | Owner | `A`, fee, pause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flexible | Spreadless | Spreadless may change them, `A` only by ramp |
| Fixed | none | frozen for good |
| Undecided | the creator | the creator may change them, and should decide |

Every offer, withdrawal, accept and renounce appears in your
[Activity](/docs/guides/portfolio) history under the **Ownership** filter.
