Spreadless is an automated market maker (AMM) for **stablecoins and other
close-to-parity assets**, built on [Stellar](https://stellar.org) and its
Soroban smart-contract platform. It exists to solve one specific, expensive
problem: the gap between the price a trade is quoted at and the amount that
actually lands in your account — the *spread*.

On a conventional AMM, that gap widens fast as your order grows. Spreadless
prices trades with a **StableSwap invariant** tuned for assets that are meant to
trade near the same value, so swapping one dollar-stablecoin for another moves
the price only fractionally — even for large orders — as long as the pool stays
reasonably balanced.

## What you can do

- **Swap** one stablecoin for another with near-zero slippage on balanced pools.
- **Provide liquidity** with a *single* asset — no manual 50/50 pairing — and
  earn a share of trading fees.
- **Withdraw** back into any single supported asset in the pool at any time.

## Why it reads differently to a typical DEX

Most decentralized exchanges were built for volatile, uncorrelated token pairs.
That machinery — constant-product curves, two-sided liquidity, unpredictable
price impact — is a poor fit when the two things you're trading are both meant to
be worth a dollar. Spreadless is built the other way around: it *assumes*
correlation and turns that assumption into tighter pricing, simpler deposits, and
execution you can preview before you sign.
